A closer desktop companion, with character-side conversation, personal ways of addressing you, and expressions that stay with the conversation.

## Conversation

- Open a compact input beside the character using **Talk**. It follows the pet, while history and settings remain separate windows.
- Set your preferred name and choose friends, romantic partners, a custom relationship, or your existing persona. Each character keeps its own settings; affection does not need to be enabled.
- Conversation gestures play once and hold their expression while the reply is visible. Idle and hover reactions no longer interrupt them. Click-only mode stays manual.
- Bubbles remain visible while voice is being generated or played. Voice failures leave the written reply intact, and starting a new message interrupts the previous voice.
- Break and focus reminders wait for the current reply to finish displaying. Old reminders do not pile up.
- Missing or invalid expression cues use a neutral pose instead of guessing from words such as "happy" inside "not happy".
- Replies support streaming, stopping, retrying failed requests, and complete paragraphs in longer messages.

## Setup And Characters

- Service presets for DeepSeek, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Qwen, SiliconFlow, OpenRouter, Groq, Ollama and LM Studio, plus custom compatible endpoints.
- Retrieve a model list or enter a model name manually. The temperature parameter can be disabled for models that do not accept it.
- Native paste shortcuts and editable-field context menus work in settings and chat, including password fields.
- Settings are organized into General, Character, Chat and Voice, with a fixed save bar and confirmation before discarding changes.
- Import character folders or ZIP files with a preview. Each character has separate persona settings and conversation history.
- Custom voice services support JSON POST and Query GET; voice tests also check whether the returned audio can play.

Existing character packs and saved preferences remain compatible. Voice, affection and saved chat history remain off by default. Names and relationship settings are saved locally and included in the persona sent to your selected model service when you chat. Model and voice service settings are shared across characters.

Chat compatibility is OpenAI Chat Completions, not every provider's native protocol. Model access, reply style and voice availability depend on your configured service. Characters use their existing sprite expressions; this update does not add Live2D or microphone input.
