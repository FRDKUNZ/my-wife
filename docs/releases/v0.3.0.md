Shared memories you control, and small everyday interactions beside your desktop.

## Shared Memories

- A new **Memories** tab keeps a separate list for each character. Add, edit or delete notes, then save. Up to 20 notes of 240 characters each.
- Off by default. Nothing is automatically extracted from your conversations, and saving notes makes no model request.
- When enabled, the list accompanies your messages to the selected model service. Turning it off stops attaching the list but preserves the local notes. Clearing the list and saving removes those notes from the character's saved state.
- Saved chat history and shared memories are independent. Deleting a note does not remove references already present in chat history or recall data already sent to a service. Notes are stored as local text; do not use them for secrets.
- Edits remain available if saving fails. Changing or removing memories cancels a pending reply that used the old list.

## Touch Responses

- In automatic expression mode, touching the head or long-pressing, tapping the body, and putting the character down after dragging give different local responses.
- Built-in characters have distinct short lines and can use your preferred name. Responses use existing character expressions, with no model calls, TTS or affection gain.
- Short lines never replace visible replies or reminders. Touch gestures do not interrupt conversations or voice playback. Turn off **General > Touch responses** for gesture-only behavior.
- Click-to-change expression mode stays manual. Dragging now restores the selected expression instead of resetting it. A double-click in manual mode no longer swallows the first click after switching modes.

Existing character packs, preferences and service configuration remain compatible. Memories, voice, affection and chat-history persistence are off by default. Accurate recall and reply style depend on the configured model. This release uses existing sprite graphics, not Live2D, and does not add microphone input.

新增独立的“共同记忆”页，每个角色拥有自己的清单，可填写、编辑、删除，最多 20 条、每条 240 字，保存后生效。默认关闭，不自动从对话中提取，也不为整理记忆额外调用模型。开启后，清单会随对话发送给所选模型；关闭保留本地内容但不再附加到新请求。清单与聊天记录独立，删除清单不会删除聊天中的既有内容或撤回服务商已收到的数据。清单以本地文本保存，不适合存放秘密，模型也不保证准确回忆。
