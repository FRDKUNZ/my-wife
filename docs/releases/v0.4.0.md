Voice controls beside your companion, with longer replies and easier interruptions.

## Voice Controls

- See whether voice is preparing or playing beside the pet, in Quick Input and in Full Chat.
- Stop during synthesis or playback. The written reply stays available.
- Replay the current character's latest successful reply without generating another model answer or increasing affection.
- Long replies are read in segments instead of stopping at the former 500-character system-voice or 800-character external-voice limit.
- Sending a new message, changing characters, clearing chat or changing voice settings stops the current voice and cancels pending synthesis or audio downloads. Late responses cannot restart the stopped voice.
- Voice controls stay separate from message sending, so you can type and send the next message while a reply is spoken.

Voice is still off by default. Replay uses your current voice service; external services receive a request for each segment and may charge for it. Stopping cannot recall text already sent or guarantee a refund. No microphone input, recording or new automatic model calls are added. Existing characters, shared memories, manual expressions and saved preferences remain compatible.
